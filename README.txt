HOW TO ADD A VACANCY
1. Open index.html in a text/code editor.
2. Find the section id="careers" and the comment "TO PUBLISH A VACANCY".
3. Duplicate the article with class="vacancy-card".
4. Remove the hidden attribute from the copied article.
5. Replace the sample department, location, employment type, job title, description and application email subject.
6. When publishing one or more vacancies, update or remove the "There are no vacancies published" message.

CV SUBMISSION IMPORTANT NOTE
The CV form validates the candidate details and consent, then prepares an email addressed to CSEC.MGT.CONSULTANCYSERVICES@GMAIL.COM. For security and privacy, this static website does NOT upload/store the CV on a server. The candidate must attach the CV manually in their email application before sending. A true direct-upload form, secure CV storage, automated vacancy management and an admin panel require a backend or connected form service and should be configured before production use.

OPENING LOCALLY
Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a modern browser. Internet access is only needed for Google Fonts.


VACANCY POSTING FORMAT
Each vacancy card should include: Job title, Job description, Requirements & skills, and a “Send your CV” email link. To publish a vacancy, duplicate the hidden vacancy-card article in index.html, fill in the details, update the mailto subject with the actual job title, and remove the hidden attribute. Keep the email address as CSEC.MGT.CONSULTANCYSERVICES@GMAIL.COM. The static website does not provide server-side CV storage; the candidate must attach their CV to the email.
